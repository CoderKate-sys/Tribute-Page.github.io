Margaret Hamilton

American NASA scientist, Software Engineer, Mathematician

Margarent Hamilton, second from the left, working on the Apollo program with other three software engineers.

The Life of The Margaret Hamilton

  • 1936 - Born in Paoli, Indiana, USA.
  • 1958 - Completed a Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics with a minor in Philosophy. (Earlham College)
  • 1959 - Developed software for weather prediction. (Massachusetts Institute of Technology)
  • 1960 - Led the development of on-board flight software for NASA's Apollo program. (MIT's Instrumentation Laboratory)
  • 1969 - Contributed heavily in the success of astronauts landing safely on the Moon.
  • 1976 - Co-founded a software that focused on preventing errors in software systems before they occur. (Higher Order Software)
  • 1986 - Founded Hamilton Technologies to further her work on software. (Hamilton Technologies)
  • 2016 - Awarded by President Barack Obama for her contributions to space exploration. (The Presidential Medal of Freedom)
